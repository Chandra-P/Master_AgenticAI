# Reusable Groups

## Signed-in workspace

Open **Groups** in navigation to manage reusable rosters owned by your signed-in account. On `GroupShare`, this list also includes groups explicitly shared with your verified account email. The page retains the existing palette, typography, editor and theme presets. Real accounts start without fictional groups. Branch implementation does not establish production deployment.

Create a group with a preset or custom theme and a suggested editable name. Names must be unique within your account, including archived groups. Your signed-in identity is the owner; each additional adult needs a distinct preferred alias. Email and first/last name are optional and private to the owner. Do not enter children's names/photos. Groups save through the authenticated backend and load on navigation/refresh across devices. **Refresh saved groups** reloads the list during plan creation. Collaborative editing and realtime group subscriptions are not included.

## Sharing and opening details — connected workspace only

Click the body of a group card, or focus its **View group …** button and press Enter/Space, to open **Group details** without entering edit mode. The owner can use **Edit group** or the independent pencil button to edit. Demo cards retain their existing behavior and do not receive this sharing/details flow.

Groups start private, including records created before this feature. In details, the owner reviews the roster and chooses **Share with members**. Added adults can then see the group when they sign in with a matching, verified email address. No account-directory lookup, invitation email, or outbound message is sent. Names, aliases and contact IDs cannot identify an account for sharing. Adults without an email remain roster aliases; add their account email before sharing with them. Email comparison trims whitespace and ignores case, without provider-specific address rewriting.

Members see the group name, theme, owner alias, adult aliases and archive status. They cannot edit, archive, remove, change sharing, or use that view-only roster to create plans. Emails and first/last names are stripped from member API responses. The owner alone retains management controls and plan creation with the roster. **Stop sharing**, removing a contact's email, changing it, removing that adult, or deleting the group removes corresponding member access on the next authorized request. Archived shared groups remain visible under Archived, with view-only access.

Saving an already shared roster updates access for the saved email list; the editor explicitly explains this before saving. Verified identity is checked anew on each request from Supabase's server-returned `email_confirmed_at`, never editable user metadata. A changed/unverified account email no longer matches. Connected group lists and open details refresh on focus and every 15 seconds while visible; details are fetched fresh on opening and cleared on failed authorization. This is polling, not instantaneous revocation of information already viewed.

**Plan with this group** preselects the roster. You can also choose/create a group during manual plan creation or AI-draft review. Inline group creation preserves the plan draft. A saved group remains even if you cancel plan creation. By default, creating the plan grants access only to you. To share it, review the account-email recipients and explicitly check **Share this plan with group members** before creating a manual or reviewed AI plan. A verified matching account receives normal participant access (view/chat/vote) and one in-app notification on its next sign-in or refresh. Aliases without account emails are shown as unable to receive automatic access. No email is sent, and the AI generation request never contains the group's contact emails.

For a previously created plan, open **Add member → Share with group**, choose an owned active group, review recipients and approve. This adds plan-sharing approvals without rewriting the original roster snapshot. Pending recipients appear there and can be revoked before joining; use **Remove access** for people already in the plan. Removal blocks old approvals from silently rejoining the account. Only a newly reviewed sharing action can restore access. The existing individual-account and private-link controls remain available; a valid private link is a separate joining method and must still be disabled if it should no longer be usable.

The notification bell appears only in the connected member workspace. Open it to see shared plans, mark a notice as read, or open the plan. Read state persists. Notifications are private to the account, omit inaccessible/deleted plans, refresh on focus/open and poll every 15 seconds while visible. At most the latest 200 notices per account are retained. Delivery requires signing in or refreshing the app; there is no email or push delivery.

A plan keeps an immutable snapshot of the group name/theme and aliases. Contact IDs, emails and legal-name fields are not copied into the public plan or assistant inputs. Approved recipient emails are kept separately in owner-authorized private sharing records, including revoked approvals; no automatic purge was introduced. Deleting the group does not cancel that plan's previously approved recipients. Later group edits, archive/restore, stopping group visibility or removing the reusable group leave existing plan participants and approved plan recipients unchanged. Manage or revoke those separately in the plan. Archived groups cannot be selected for a new plan or newly approved sharing action. Minimal aliases already captured in existing plans remain part of those plans.

## Storage and access

Group records are persisted inside the existing transactional PostgreSQL JSON state aggregate (`tagalong_state`), accessed only through authenticated FastAPI endpoints. Owner identity is assigned server-side. Reads allow the owner or an explicitly shared verified-email match and return role-specific projections; all writes and roster selection for plan creation remain owner-only. Revisions reject stale edits and sharing approvals. Existing state without a groups key remains valid; records without a `shared` flag are private. No SQL migration, credential, or external provisioning change is needed.

API: GET/POST `/v1/groups`, GET/PATCH/DELETE `/v1/groups/{id}`, POST `/v1/groups/{id}/sharing` with `shared` and `expected_revision`. Updates/deletions require `expected_revision`; manual creation and generated-draft acceptance accept paired `group_id` and `expected_group_revision`. Group read projections include server-derived `role`, `ownerName` and `shared`; these fields cannot be used to forge write access. Group audit metadata contains actor/group IDs, action and timestamp (including share/unshare), without contact fields. Existing database clients remain unable to read the private state aggregate directly.

Account changes/sign-out clear group state and ignore late responses. A backend failure shows an error instead of silently substituting local demo data. Group operations make no AI calls. There is a limit of 100 saved groups per owner and 12 additional adults per group. No age-detection mechanism is claimed: follow the adult-only instructions and use synthetic data for testing.

## Local demo

The demo stays separate, with fictional Alex and browser-local `tagalong-groups-v1` data. It retains the theme/member/editor/archive/removal flow, synthetic seeds and local plan snapshots. It neither imports into an account automatically nor affects shared plans. To explore it, switch explicitly through **Account → Explore the demo**. Connected groups do not load demo contacts.
