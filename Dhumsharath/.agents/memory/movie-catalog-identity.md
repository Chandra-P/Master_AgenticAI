---
name: Movie catalog identity
description: How to interpret duplicate films versus distinct selectable language versions.
---

Consolidate spelling, subtitle, and streaming-label aliases of the same film, but preserve remakes, sequels, and explicitly distinct language versions.

**Why:** The requested pool should give each distinct selectable movie one entry without erasing language versions. A translated display title alone is not evidence of a separate version.

**How to apply:** Review year, cast, hints, and language context together rather than deduplicating by title alone. Preserve retained IDs and prefer explicit language labels when otherwise identical titles represent different versions.
