## Learnings

Performance drivers: Direct mail accounted for 138 verified activations and display for 92. Channel composition is an observed association, not causal lift.

Audience: Existing members supplied 138 activations and prospective members 92; no age-level or income-level outcome breakdown was measured.

Channels: Direct mail: 138 activations within 60 days of mail/drop date; display: 92 within 30 days of exposure. Total attributed spend: USD 20908. Channel-specific spend and exposure counts are in Channel Results. Financial return is unavailable, so channel ROI cannot be calculated.

Lessons: Deduplicated outcomes and documented attribution prevent inflated activation totals. Avoid assuming association proves causality.

## Recommendations

Proposed tests, not proven performance improvements: Test clearer eligibility language and a segmented follow-up cadence before changing allocation; validate results in a controlled experiment.

Best-practice guidance: Validate tracking before scaling. Test segmented follow-up with a randomized holdout. Treat observed channel associations as hypotheses, not causal lift. Never claim unmeasured audience or channel ROI.

## Measurement Notes and Limitations

Sum of posted account balances at opening (USD); not current balances, net new deposits, or revenue.

Observational attribution; no randomized control group. Campaign-level acquisition cost is not incremental ROI.

Reporting cohort: 2026-Q2. Direct mail: 60 days from mail/drop date; display: 30 days from exposure. Include only conversions on/after the anchor and no later than the channel window boundary. Both windows are fully observed as of 2026-06-30. De-duplicate activations across channels; outcomes are pre-assigned to one eligible channel in this synthetic corpus.

Map locations are fictional aggregated outcomes at approximate city centers, not customer addresses. Rates use channel-specific denominators and must not be compared as causal channel lift. All institutions, data and findings in this demonstration are synthetic.
